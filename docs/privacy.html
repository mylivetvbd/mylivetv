Privacy Policy — My Live TV

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the My Live TV Android application ("the App") handles information. The App is a free IPTV player that lets users watch publicly available live TV streams.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require registration and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect anonymous usage data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — may use device identifiers to show ads. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous usage statistics such as number of active users, country, session length). See Firebase Privacy.

2. How Information Is Used

Anonymous data is used only to understand how many people use the App, improve stability and features, and show advertisements that keep the App free.

3. Content

The App is a player. It plays publicly available streams from the internet. The App does not host, upload, or store any video content on its own servers.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The App is intended for general audiences.

5. Data Security

Since the App collects no personal data itself, there is no personal data stored by us. Third-party services (Google) handle their data according to their own security standards.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official website:
https://mylivetvbd.github.io/mylivetv/
